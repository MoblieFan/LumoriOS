# LumoriOS (LmOS)

LumoriOS (short name: LmOS) is an alternative operating system designed to replace AndroidOS on mobile devices.  
It is built for **long-term support, security, and freedom**, aiming to last for decades without forced obsolescence.

---

## ✨ Vision
- Provide a **lightweight, secure, and open-source OS** for smartphones and tablets.
- Support devices from **Samsung, Google Pixel, Xiaomi, Huawei**, and more.
- Deliver **10+ years of updates**, inspired by PostmarketOS but branded as LumoriOS.
- Focus on **user freedom**: no AI bloat, only essential Google Services if desired.

---

## 🔎 Key Features
- **[Linux base](ca://s?q=LumoriOS_Linux_base)** → built on PostmarketOS (Alpine Linux) or OpenSUSE ARM.  
- **[Mobile UI](ca://s?q=LumoriOS_mobile_UI)** → Phosh, Plasma Mobile, or Lomiri for touch devices.  
- **[Android compatibility](ca://s?q=LumoriOS_Waydroid_support)** → Waydroid/Anbox container to run Android apps.  
- **[Security](ca://s?q=LumoriOS_security_model)** → hardened kernel, SELinux, sandboxing.  
- **[Long-term updates](ca://s?q=LumoriOS_long_term_support)** → target of 10 years support per device.  

---

## 📊 Supported Devices
- Samsung Galaxy Tab series  
- Google Pixel phones  
- Xiaomi Redmi / Mi series  
- Huawei devices (with ARM64 support)  
- Community ports for other ARM devices  

---

## 🚀 Getting Started
1. Download the latest **LumoriOS image (.img)** for your device.  
2. Flash it via fastboot/Odin (depending on vendor).  
3. Boot into LumoriOS and configure your environment.  
4. Optionally install **Waydroid** for Android app compatibility.  

---

## ⚠️ Limitations
- Bootloader unlock required (not available on all devices).  
- Some proprietary drivers (GPU, modem, camera) may need reverse engineering.  
- Banking apps and DRM services may not work without Google certification.
